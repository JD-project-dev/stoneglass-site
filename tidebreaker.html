Tidebreaker

Race the water, not just the track.

A jet ski racer on six stretches of living water. Thread the buoy gates, launch off real waves and land tricks for boost.

See it on the water

Touch, tilt or controllerSteer with the touch stick, tilt your phone or pick up a game controller. Optimized for foldable and standard phones.

What's on the water

  • Living waterThe waves you see are the ones you ride. Wakes push you around, and the swell and tide change from lap to lap.
  • Big air, clean landingsFlips and barrel rolls with just the stick, spins, handstands and stand-ups. Land clean for full points and a boost.
  • Race the packThread the red and yellow buoy gates for power and fight up to seven rivals to the line.
  • Sunset to stormFour times of day and three weathers, from a glassy lagoon at noon to a storm lit up by lightning.
  • Three cups, four ways to rideChampionship across the Breakwater, Tempest and Tidebreaker cups, plus Time Trial against your own ghost, Stunt and Free Ride.
  • Pick your riderJuno, Bram, Piper and Dario: an all-rounder, a heavyweight, a trickster and a carver, with ten colors and four liveries to earn.
  • Plays anywhereOptimized for foldable and standard phones, plays offline, controller ready.
