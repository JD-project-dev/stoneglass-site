Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy covers Crime Spree, the Android game, and this website. Both are made by Stoneglass Labs LLC ("we", "us").

The short version

  • Your game progress and settings stay on your phone. We don't run game servers, and we never receive them.
  • Google Drive backup is optional. It saves to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, and we never receive the save or your email address.
  • Reward ads are optional and come from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash-reporting tools, no location permission and no chat.
  • We don't sell personal information.

Data that stays on your phone

Your progress, inventory and settings are stored on your device. We never receive them. You can remove them by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you turn on backup, you sign in with Google through Google Play services. The game asks only for permission to use a hidden app folder in your Google Drive. Your saves are stored there and the game keeps the last five. We don't receive your saves or your email address. Google handles your sign-in and Drive storage under its own privacy policy.

To delete your backups, open Google Drive, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Crime Spree and choose to delete its hidden app data.

Reward ads (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a reward ad for an in-game bonus, up to 25 a day. Ads are served by Google AdMob. When ads are requested or shown, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • an approximate location based on your IP address
  • how you interact with ads
  • diagnostic information

Google uses this data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

The game can remind you when turf income is full, a streak is about to break or free chips are ready. These reminders are scheduled on your phone; there's no push server. Android asks your permission first, and you can turn them off in your settings.

Purchases

If you buy something in the game, Google Play processes the payment. We only receive confirmation of the purchase, never your payment details.

This website

We don't use analytics or advertising cookies on this site.

What we don't collect

Crime Spree has no accounts, analytics, crash reporting, location permission, contacts access, chat or user-generated content.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent personal information. The only third parties involved are Google, for the optional Drive backup and the optional ads described above.

Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your phone until you delete it. Drive backups stay in your Google Drive until you delete them. Our Delete your data page has the steps for each.

Security

Data sent for Drive backup and for ads is encrypted in transit.

Children

Crime Spree is meant for older players and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date at the top.
