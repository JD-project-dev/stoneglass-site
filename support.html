Crime Spree

Support

Answers to the usual questions. If yours isn't here, email us and a real person will get back to you.

When does Crime Spree come out?

It's coming soon to Android. The launch date will be posted on this site.

Will it run on my phone?

Crime Spree is optimized for foldable and standard Android phones. On foldables it works on both the cover and inner screens, in portrait and landscape.

Can I play with a controller?

Yes. It works with all standard game controllers. Connect the controller to your phone first, then open the game.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Crime Spree plays offline. The internet is only used if you turn on Google Drive backup or choose to watch a reward ad.

How do I back up my progress?

Turn on Google Drive backup in the game's settings and sign in with your Google account. Your saves go to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, and the game keeps your last five.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?
  1. On your old phone, run a Google Drive backup.
  2. Install Crime Spree on your new phone.
  3. Turn on Google Drive backup with the same Google account and restore your latest save.
Are there ads?

Only if you want them. Reward ads are optional, you choose when to watch one, and there's a limit of 25 a day.

Who is the game for?

Crime Spree has action violence and a casino played with in-game chips, so it's meant for older players. We expect a Mature 17+ rating.
