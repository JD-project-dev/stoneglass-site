About the studio

Stoneglass Labs is an independent game studio. We make fast, loud games for the phone in your pocket.

Our first game is Crime Spree, an open-world shooter where four factions fight over one city. We build and test every update on real devices, from foldables to standard phones, and we play every build ourselves.
