Crime Spree

Delete your data

Crime Spree doesn't have accounts, and we don't hold your game data. Here's how to remove what's on your phone, in your Google Drive and in your ad settings.

Game progress on your phone

  1. Open your phone's Settings, then Apps.
  2. Choose Crime Spree, then Storage.
  3. Tap Clear storage. Uninstalling the game also removes it.

Google Drive backups

  1. Open Google Drive and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Manage apps and find Crime Spree.
  3. Choose to delete its hidden app data.

Advertising ID

Open your phone's Settings and find the Ads section (often under Privacy or Google). From there you can reset or delete your advertising ID.
